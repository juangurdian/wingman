# Code of Conduct

Wingman is a small project that wants to grow into a friendly one. Everyone who takes part — in issues, pull requests, Discussions, or anywhere else the project is represented — is expected to follow this code.

## Our standards

Helpful behavior:

- Being welcoming to newcomers and patient with questions
- Giving and accepting constructive, specific feedback
- Assuming good faith and focusing on what is best for users
- Crediting other people's work and ideas

Not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome attention
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission
- Spam, bad-faith arguments, or repeatedly derailing discussions

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that don't follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors whose behavior is harmful.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through GitHub ([@juangurdian](https://github.com/juangurdian)). Reports are kept confidential. Security vulnerabilities go through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.
